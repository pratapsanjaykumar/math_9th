Key Concepts

Foundations of Cartesian Geometry

  • ➥
    Coordinate System:

    A system to locate points on a plane using a pair of numbers.

  • ➥
    Axes:

    Two perpendicular lines, the x-axis (horizontal) and the y-axis (vertical).

  • ➥
    Origin:

    The point where the axes intersect (0, 0).

Coordinates

Positioning on a 2D Grid

➥

An ordered pair (x, y) representing the position of a point.

➥
x-coordinate (abscissa):

Perpendicular distance of the point from the y-axis.

➥
y-coordinate (ordinate):

Perpendicular distance of the point from the x-axis.
